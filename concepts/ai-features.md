# AI Features (Einstein & Agentforce)

## Core Idea
Marketing Cloud Next ships with generative AI (Agentforce) and predictive AI (Einstein) features that draft campaigns, optimize send times, filter bot clicks, and score engagement — all protected by the Einstein Trust Layer.

## Detailed Explanation

### Setup AI (Agentforce)
1. Set up **Salesforce Foundations** (required for agent templates)
2. Set up **Einstein Generative AI** (and Agentforce Employee Agent)
3. Enable Agentforce Employee Agent + user permissions
4. Enable Marketing Cloud features (Setup → "Einstein & Agentforce")

### Agentforce Agents & Standard Actions

| Agent | Standard Actions |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Campaign Creation** | Draft Campaign Brief, Refine Campaign Preview, Save Campaign Brief, Create Campaign from Brief, Save Campaign, Identify Record by Name, Summarize Campaign, Generate Campaign Insights, Identify Business Unit |
| **Content Builder** | Draft Content, Create Section with Content, Create Section |
| **Journey Decisioning** (Engagement+) | Select Journey, Create Journey Content |

### Einstein Predictive AI Features

| Feature | What it does | Edition |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Send Time Optimization (STO)** | Sends email at optimal time per recipient | Growth (global model required) / Advanced |
| **Metrics Guard** | Filters bot/non-human clicks & opens | Growth + Advanced |
| **Engagement Scoring** | Contact-level engagement scores | Advanced only |
| **Engagement Frequency** | Determines optimal contact frequency | Advanced only |

> **Three predictive AI capabilities** (like MCE): **Engagement Frequency**, **Engagement Scoring**, and **Send Time Optimization**. ⚠️ **Engagement Frequency and Engagement Scoring are Advanced only** — not Growth.

### Using Predictive AI Insights
You can use the insights generated by these features for **audience segmentation** and **flow decisioning**:
- To make their attributes available for decisioning in a flow, you must first configure the **required data graph**.
- Once configured, use fields such as **Email Engagement Frequency** or **Engagement Score** to create branches with a **Decision element**.
- Alternatively, use these **DMO fields as criteria** when building a segment.

### Engagement Frequency (deep dive)
Uses predictive AI to determine **how often each contact should receive an email** — maximising engagement while reducing **email fatigue and unsubscribes**.

- **Analyses up to 90 days** of email activity: sends, opens, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes, spam complaints, and **sending patterns**.
- Compares each individual's behaviour at different sending frequencies with **individuals who show similar patterns**, then calculates an **ideal frequency range**.
- ⚠️ Trained **only on your organisation's data**; scores refreshed **weekly**.
- **Use:** as segment criteria or in a Decision element — e.g., reduce communications to over-mailed individuals, increase to those who could be engaged more.
- **Classification field:** the **Email Engagement Classification** field in the **Email Engagement Frequency DMO** assigns each individual to one of **four categories**: **Saturated · Almost Saturated · On Target · Under Saturated**.

### Engagement Scoring (deep dive)
Uses predictive AI to estimate **how likely each individual is to engage with future marketing communications**.

- **Analyses up to 90 days** of historical behaviour, placing **greater emphasis on the most recent activity**.
- Inputs include email sends, opens, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes, **subscription history**, and **website interactions**.
- Generates **three likelihood predictions** per individual: likelihood to **open**, likelihood to **click**, and likelihood to **remain subscribed**.
- **Personas** (based on predicted open/click likelihood):

| Persona | Open | Click |
|---------|------|-------|
| **Loyalists** | High | High |
| **Selective Subscribers** | Low | High |
| **Window Shoppers** | High | Low |
| **Win Back / Dormant** | Low | Low |

- **Use personas for strategy:** send surveys to understand selective subscribers, engage window shoppers via alternative channels, place dormant subscribers into a dedicated win-back campaign.
- Each likelihood prediction is also grouped into one of **four tiers**: **Most Likely · More Likely · Less Likely · Least Likely** — usable in a Decision element to route individuals.

### Send Time Optimization (deep dive)
Uses predictive AI to determine **when each individual is most likely to engage with an email** — rather than sending to everyone at the same time.

- **Analyses up to 90 days** of email engagement history: sends, opens, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes, spam complaints, **plus the timestamps** of those events.
- Applied to a **Send Email Message** flow element, MC Next times each email per the recipient's predicted engagement pattern (one person morning, another evening).
- Predictions updated **approximately once a week**.
- ⚠️ Available in **both Growth and Advanced** editions, and **must be enabled in Setup**.
- **Setup:** add the **Send Time Optimization DMO** to your data graph (using the required structure), then select the **Hourly Scores by Week** field. The Send Email Message element uses this field to determine each recipient's optimal send time.
- **Optimization window:** within the element, enable STO and define a window from **2 hours up to 1 week**; MC Next sends at each recipient's predicted optimal time within that window.

### Key Setup Notes
- **STO / Scoring / Frequency** all require an identity resolution ruleset with **Individual** as primary DMO (must be one of first two rulesets).
- **STO:** Setup → "Send Time" → Einstein Send Time Optimization with Global Models → Enable. Analyzes past 90 days.
- **Metrics Guard:** Setup → "Messaging" → Email Feature Settings → turn on. Score 0–100% (lower = more likely real).
- **Engagement Scoring:** Setup → "Scoring" → Einstein Engagement Scoring.
- **Engagement Frequency:** Setup → "Frequency" → Einstein Engagement Frequency.

### Einstein Data Usage
- **Agentforce agents** use generative AI models (Yes Generative AI, No global model)
- **STO** and **Metrics Guard** use global models (required in Growth edition)
- **Engagement Scoring/Frequency** (Advanced) use neither generative AI nor global models

### Einstein Trust Layer (AI Security Architecture)
All agentic/conversational AI is protected by the **Einstein Trust Layer**, a secure architecture built natively into the platform:

| Mechanism | What it does |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Custom guardrails** | Brand guidelines, FAQs, tone-of-voice ground the agent and keep it on-script |
| **Toxicity detection** | Blocks hateful/abusive/profane content and deflects prompt-injection attempts |
| **Dynamic grounding** | Answers are grounded in your Data 360 (not the public internet); prevents hallucinations |
| **Subagent/action fences** | Defines what the agent may discuss; off-limits topics politely deflected |
| **Data masking** | Detects PII (credit cards, phones, addresses) and replaces with placeholders before sending to the LLM; unmasks on response |
| **Zero data retention** | LLM providers don't retain prompts/data/responses; your data never trains outside models |
| **Encryption** | In transit (TLS) and at rest (Data 360 logs/profiles) |
| **Audit trail** | Logs every interaction (masked data, toxicity scores, grounding data) for compliance |
| **Human-in-the-loop / escalation** | Auto-escalate frustrated or complex conversations to a live rep |

## Common Pitfalls / Misconceptions
⚠️ Einstein Engagement Scoring and Engagement Frequency are **Advanced edition only**.
⚠️ STO requires the identity resolution ruleset with Individual as primary DMO, and it must be in the **first two rulesets**.
⚠️ Metrics Guard score is counterintuitive: **lower = more likely real** (higher = machine-generated).
⚠️ Disabling STO doesn't remove it from existing sends (only new messages).
⚠️ **Engagement Frequency is trained only on your org's data** and refreshes **weekly**.
⚠️ **Engagement Scoring emphasises recent activity** — it's not a flat 90-day average.
⚠️ **STO needs the Send Time Optimization DMO added to the data graph** (Hourly Scores by Week field) before it works.
⚠️ STO's optimization window ranges from **2 hours to 1 week**.
